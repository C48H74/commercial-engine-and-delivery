## Before the meeting (preparation)

- [ ] **Objectives defined** — what must be clear at the end
- [ ] **Agenda sent** in advance
- [ ] **Key parties invited** — including whoever can block
- [ ] **Materials ready** — charter, scope, milestone schedule, RACI
- [ ] **Tone decided** — formal, informal or collaborative
- [ ] **Sales handoff document** reviewed (if applicable)

> A kickoff without prepared material turns into a presentation meeting. It does not count.

---

## Agenda with timings (75 minutes)

| Time | Block | What happens | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 min | **Welcome and introductions** | who is who; if people don't know each other, introduce them | `[name]` |
| 15 min | **Project overview** | purpose, expected results, why it matters to the business | `[name]` |
| 15 min | **Scope and objectives** | what is inside **and outside**; success criteria | `[name]` |
| 10 min | **Roles and responsibilities** | RACI: who does, who approves, who is informed | `[name]` |
| 10 min | **Schedule and milestones** | dates, dependencies, **client dependencies** | `[name]` |
| 10 min | **Communication plan** | who receives what, when and through which channel | `[name]` |
| 10 min | **Risks** | main risks, assumptions and constraints | `[name]` |
| 15 min | **Questions and answers** | doubts and concerns | everyone |
| 10 min | **Next steps** | actions, owners and deadlines | `[name]` |

**Facilitation tips:** arrive early · encourage participation from quieter people · be clear and concise · promote collaboration · **follow up in writing afterwards**.

## Kickoff completion checklist

- [ ] Everyone knows **why** the project exists
- [ ] Everyone knows **what is outside** the scope
- [ ] Each participant knows their role and what is expected of them
- [ ] Client dependencies are written down and have an owner
- [ ] Milestone dates have been confirmed out loud (not just presented)
- [ ] Communication channels and frequency are agreed
- [ ] Actions have an owner and a deadline
- [ ] Minutes sent in writing the same day
