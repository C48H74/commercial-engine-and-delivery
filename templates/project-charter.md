## The 7 most common errors in this document

1. Starting the project **without** a charter
2. Not defining objectives (or defining them without a measurable criterion)
3. Poorly defined scope — **without the list of what is left out**
4. Not identifying the stakeholders
5. Not including milestones
6. **Confusing the charter with the project plan** (the charter authorises; the plan details)
7. Not documenting assumptions and constraints
