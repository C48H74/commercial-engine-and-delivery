## Good practices for this report

- **Clarity and concision** — the reader has 3 minutes
- **Same format every time** — the reader knows where to look
- **Regularity** — delaying the report delays the decision
- **Trends and variances** — not only today's snapshot
- **Focus on decisions** — not on raw data
- **Highlight what needs action** — the rest is an annex
