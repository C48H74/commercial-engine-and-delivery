## Sales-to-delivery handoff — 6 dimensions to confirm

- [ ] Agreed scope (what is in and what is not)
- [ ] Client expectations (what they expect and when)
- [ ] Original pain **and the number** they used to justify the purchase
- [ ] Parties and roles (who decides, who uses, who can block)
- [ ] Known risks (technical, adoption, timing)
- [ ] Success criteria agreed with the **economic buyer**

> Without these six items, the first month of delivery redoes the sale — and usually with less information than the salesperson had.
