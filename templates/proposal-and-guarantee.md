## Checklist before sending

- [ ] Is the pain **in the client's words**, with name and date?
- [ ] Is the promised result **quantified** (or marked as an estimate)?
- [ ] Are there **three tiers** and is the **recommended one marked**?
- [ ] Has the unit economics been calculated for all three?
- [ ] Has the guarantee passed the **exposure mathematics**?
- [ ] Does the anchoring come **before** the price?
- [ ] Is the paper process mapped **with dates**?
- [ ] Does the "not for you" section exist?
- [ ] Is the proof cited **authorised** by the client?
- [ ] No number without a source or marked as an estimate?
- [ ] Is there a **concrete next step** with a date?
