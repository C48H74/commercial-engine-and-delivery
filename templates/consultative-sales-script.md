# Script — consultative selling with profile qualification

> **Principle:** before selling, **check the voltage of the socket**. Closing a deal the client cannot implement generates churn, complaints and reputational damage — worse than not selling.

## Objective

Run sales meetings focused on **diagnosis**, **practical usefulness** and **disqualification** of clients outside the ideal profile (ICP).

## Required inputs

| Input | Description |
|---|---|
| **ICP and minimum criteria** | minimum infrastructure, maturity and budget |
| **Diagnostic script** | situational and pain-identification questions (use SPIN, file 03) |
| **Solution matrix and limits** | what you deliver, what you do not deliver, and where it does not work |

---

## Operational steps

### 1. Segmentation in the narrative
Open the interaction by addressing **specific challenges of the client's niche** — contextualised communication, not generic.
> This comes **before** any product customisation. The message is the primary tool for selecting the audience.

### 2. Diagnostic investigation
Logical questions about the buyer's **infrastructure, routine and maturity**: available team, systems, processes, history, constraints.
Anchor question: *"what needs to exist on your side for this to work?"*

### 3. Apply logical empathy
Show understanding of the operational pains and **clarify the economic impact of inaction**. Empathy (listening, welcoming) **plus** logic (numbers, consequence) — both, not one.

### 4. Fit check
Compare the diagnostic answers with the product's **success criteria matrix**. This is where the sale is cleared or blocked.

### 5. Steer the solution

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| **Fit** | make tangible how the product fixes the problem **and demonstrate the expected return** |
| **Not a fit** | **communicate the incompatibility transparently and refuse the close** |

**How to refuse without losing the relationship:**
> "From what you've told me, in your setup this won't work — you don't have [prerequisite]. If I sold it to you today, in three months you'd be frustrated and I'd have a dissatisfied client. I'd rather tell you what would need to exist first. If you like, I can refer you to [alternative/partner]."

---

## Outputs

- **Qualification record** filled in with the client's maturity data.
- **Personalised value proposal** for their operational reality **or** a formal, justified **disqualification record**.

---

## Decision criteria

| Verdict | Criterion |
|---|---|
| **Sale cleared** | the client has the **operational** and **financial** capacity to implement and obtain a measurable result |
| **Sale blocked / refer a partner** | does not meet the technical or budget prerequisites — **selling is forbidden** |

**Non-negotiable institutional rule:** selling outside the ICP **for financial volume** must be treated as a process failure, not an achievement. If your team is paid only on volume, this script will not survive — **review the compensation** before demanding the discipline.

---

## Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Accepting any request to hit a target | churn at link 5 of the diagnosis |
| Diagnosing only the budget and not the operation | client buys and does not use it |
| Customising the product before testing the narrative | high cost for an untested hypothesis |
| Empathy without numbers (or numbers without empathy) | either cold or salesy |
| Refusing without explanation and without an alternative | loses the relationship — the goal is to keep the door open |
