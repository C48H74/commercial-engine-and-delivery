# Anti-patterns checklist

> **How to use:** go through it before delivering. Each ticked item is a defect to fix — not an observation to mention. If you find more than three, the deliverable is not ready.

---

## 1. Trust and evidence

- [ ] Invented urgency or a false deadline
- [ ] False scarcity ("last spots" when it isn't true)
- [ ] Case study or testimonial **without** the client's authorisation
- [ ] Simulation or projection presented as a real client result
- [ ] Number without a source (it should be marked "Estimate" or "Not found")
- [ ] Own number, testimonial or case **invented** instead of `[to fill in]`
- [ ] Business profile out of date (a new number contradicts the recorded one and was not updated)

## 2. Offer and price

- [ ] Offer with incomplete elements (result, timeframe, mechanism, proof, risk, price)
- [ ] Vague phrases: "complete solution", "strategic partnership", "transforms your business"
- [ ] **A single price tier** (turns the decision into yes/no)
- [ ] Recommended tier not explicitly marked
- [ ] Tier with **negative margin** or break-even above capacity
- [ ] Guarantee without exposure calculation
- [ ] Guarantee that promises a result dependent on the client
- [ ] Discount offered before diagnosing the cause of "it's expensive"
- [ ] Price anchored to your cost, not to the client's alternative

## 3. Discovery and qualification

- [ ] Solution presented before quantifying the pain
- [ ] Friendly contact treated as a **champion** (without the 3 criteria)
- [ ] Talking to someone who cannot buy and calling it pipeline
- [ ] Only one contact in the account, with no multithreading plan
- [ ] "Ghost node" not looked for (nobody asked who else needs to agree)
- [ ] Implication question **without** offering a way out (deflates the buyer)
- [ ] Discovery turned into an interview (too much Situation)
- [ ] Presenting the solution early, cutting the implication steps
- [ ] Alarming too much — the client freezes instead of acting
- [ ] Paper process ignored (the deal dies after the "yes")

## 4. Pipeline and diagnosis

- [ ] Deal advanced a stage without meeting the gate criterion
- [ ] Changing the price when the problem is **reach** (link 1)
- [ ] Changing the offer when the problem is the **target** (link 2)
- [ ] Scaling acquisition with **capacity** saturated (link 4)
- [ ] Chasing new clients when the problem is **retention** (link 5)
- [ ] Diagnosis without **one** experiment card
- [ ] Experiment card **without a stop rule**
- [ ] Two variables changed at the same time
- [ ] Vanity metric presented as a result

## 5. Outbound and communication

- [ ] Email with a generic observation ("I noticed your company is growing")
- [ ] More than 3 sentences in the first contact
- [ ] More than one request per email
- [ ] Meeting request in the first touch
- [ ] Flattery, jargon or "I hope you're well"
- [ ] Touch sent without a real, verifiable observation
- [ ] Sequence without an honest close at touch 5

## 6. Project and scope

- [ ] Project started **without a signed project charter**
- [ ] Charter without the explicit list of what is **outside** the scope
- [ ] Verbal request accepted without a record or formal approval
- [ ] Change executed before assessing the impact on the baselines
- [ ] Estimate treated as a fixed deadline
- [ ] Planned work that is not ready yet
- [ ] Real team capacity ignored in planning
- [ ] Dependencies not declared at the kickoff
- [ ] Single delivery at the end when the product needs continuous feedback

## 7. Schedule, cost and forecast

- [ ] Baseline missing or not approved
- [ ] Variance calculated with inverted sign (`EV − PV` and `EV − AC`, in that order)
- [ ] Forecast presented as a **single number**, without a confidence range
- [ ] ETC / EAC out of date compared with actuals
- [ ] Approved changes not reflected in the baselines
- [ ] Contingency reserve used up without a baseline review
- [ ] Variance outside the limit **with no defined action**
- [ ] Status report as a data dump, without a decision requested

## 8. Risk and measurement

- [ ] Risk recorded without a **named owner**
- [ ] Risk without a cause → event → effect description
- [ ] Different probability and impact scales between risks
- [ ] No **opportunity** being treated
- [ ] Accepted risk and **ignored** (no monitoring)
- [ ] Remaining reserve insufficient for the remaining risk
- [ ] Confirmation bias: a report that only shows what confirms the view
- [ ] Correlation treated as causation
- [ ] Hawthorne effect ignored (measuring changed behaviour)
- [ ] Risk beyond authority not escalated in writing

## 9. People and rituals

- [ ] Daily meeting running past 15 minutes
- [ ] Direction meeting debating instead of deciding
- [ ] Vague update accepted ("almost done")
- [ ] Meeting ended **without** an action item with an owner
- [ ] RACI with **more than one "A"** on the same activity
- [ ] RACI with an activity **without an "R"**
- [ ] RACI convention not declared (R/A/C/I or D/A/C/I)
- [ ] Micromanagement disguised as follow-up
- [ ] Quarterly review blaming people instead of examining systems
- [ ] Known blocker not removed before asking for updates

---

## 10. Idea validation (before structuring)

- [ ] Offer, price and outbound structured **before** anyone has paid
- [ ] Validation done by praise, not by payment
- [ ] Pilot batch too large (destroys the low cost of error)
- [ ] Long test deadline (the learning loses value)
- [ ] Refusals not noted (the objection is the most valuable data)
- [ ] Sales only to friends who buy out of kindness
- [ ] Success criterion changed **after** seeing the result
- [ ] Attachment to the product: defending what was made despite feedback

## 11. Negotiation

- [ ] Open question where a closed choice was possible ("shall we close?" instead of "A or B?")
- [ ] Negotiating without a defined floor (price, timeline, scope)
- [ ] Two concessions in a row without a counterpart
- [ ] Concession treated as a gift, not as an exchange
- [ ] Client surprised with a new condition on the table
- [ ] Negative point hidden or distorted (instead of reframed transparently)
- [ ] Waiting without a forecast (timeline communicated without stages and update dates)
- [ ] Sale closed without checking whether the client's infrastructure supports the solution
- [ ] Sale outside the ICP accepted for financial volume
- [ ] Close without recapping the gains on both sides

---

## Final reading

| Items ticked | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| **0** | ready to deliver |
| **1–2** | fix before sending |
| **3–5** | the deliverable has a structural problem — review the corresponding module |
| **6+** | do not deliver: go back to the diagnosis (`references/05-commercial-engine-12-steps.md`) |
| **Any item in section 10** | stop everything: you are structuring before validating |
