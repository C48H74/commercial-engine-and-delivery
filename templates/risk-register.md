## Review ritual

| When | What is reviewed |
|---|---|
| **Weekly** (fixed agenda item) | active risks · triggers fired · new threats and opportunities · reserves |
| **Monthly** | total exposure · responses that did not work · risks to close or escalate |
| **At each milestone** | risks for the next period · risks that have ceased to exist |

**Golden rule:** a risk left in a drawer only surfaces when it becomes a problem. Including risk on the weekly agenda is what makes the team proactive instead of reactive.

---

## Register quality checklist

- [ ] Does the risk describe **cause → event → effect**, not just a vague fear?
- [ ] Does it have a **named owner** (a person, not a department)?
- [ ] Do probability and impact use the **same scale** for everyone?
- [ ] Has the **residual exposure** been assessed after the response?
- [ ] Is there an objective **trigger** for the high risks?
- [ ] Is at least **one opportunity** being treated?
- [ ] Does the remaining reserve cover the remaining risk?
- [ ] Have risks beyond my authority been **escalated in writing**?
